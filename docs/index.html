NeuralDBG

Stop guessing why your model failed. Get structured causal explanations for PyTorch training dynamics.

Why NeuralDBG?

📉 TensorBoard / W&B

Shows graphs of loss over time. You see when it failed, but you have to guess why.

❌ Manual diagnosis required

🧠 NeuralDBG

Analyzes gradients, activations, and data to tell you why it failed.

✅ "Gradient vanishing in layer 2 at step 45 (Confidence: 0.92)"

Quick Start

Wrap your training loop in one line. No dashboard setup, no cloud account.

import torch
from neuraldbg import NeuralDbg

model = MyModel()
optimizer = torch.optim.Adam(model.parameters())

# Wrap your training loop
with NeuralDbg(model) as dbg:
    for step, (x, y) in enumerate(dataloader):
        optimizer.zero_grad()
        dbg.step = step
        loss = criterion(model(x), y)
        loss.backward()
        dbg.record_loss(loss.item())
        optimizer.step()

# Get the explanation after a failure
print(dbg.explain_failure())
# >> "Gradient vanishing originated in layer 'linear1' at step 234..."

Key Features

🔍 Semantic Events

Detects transitions like "Healthy → Vanishing" or "Stable → Exploding" automatically.

🧩 Causal Reasoning

Ranks hypotheses by confidence. "Likely LR mismatch" vs "Possible data anomaly".

🛡️ 100% Local

No data leaves your machine. Privacy-first by design.

⚡ Compiler-Aware

Works seamlessly with torch.compile and distributed training.
